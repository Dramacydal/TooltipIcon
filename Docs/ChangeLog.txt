
TooltipIcon ChangeLog
================
1.0.0 (November 9, 2026)
----------------------------
- First release for WoW: Forever.
- Icons inside tooltips, in the top-left corner next to the name: spells, talents,
  buffs and debuffs, items (with comparison tooltips), toys, companion pets, mounts,
  currencies and transmog appearances. Players, NPCs and pets get their portrait.
- Items: the item properties (binding, item level, slot, Unique, Crafting Reagent,
  Cosmetic, bag size, ...) are shown next to the icon. Talents: the rank is shown next
  to the icon.
- Units: the faction and the creature type of NPCs and the owner of pets are shown next
  to the portrait; "PvP" is shown next to the name. Players: the class and the faction
  as icons on the level line (size and shift are adjustable) or on one line; optional
  class colors for player names.
- Battlegrounds and combat: the game hides most data of enemy players there. Their
  tooltips are still laid out with what the client allows (portrait, name, level, guild,
  faction, "PvP"). The option "Skip enemy players and pets in battlegrounds (Unstable)"
  leaves such tooltips to the game.
- Settings page (Esc -> Options -> AddOns -> TooltipIcon): a master switch, a checkbox for
  every tooltip category, "Name only next to the icon" for spells, items, toys,
  companion pets, mounts and units, minimum icon size, gap, icon border, round
  portraits, and a "Reset to Defaults" button. Slash command: /tti.
- Works in every client language; the settings are in English, German, French, Spanish,
  Russian and Chinese (Simplified).
- Works together with other addons that change tooltips (Leatrix Plus and others): if
  another addon keeps rewriting the lines after TooltipIcon, a warning is shown once.
